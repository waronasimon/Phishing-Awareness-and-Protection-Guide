# How to Spot Phishing - 10 Red Flags

1. **Sender email mismatch** - support@micorsoft.com not microsoft.com
2. **Urgent/threatening language** - "Act now or else"
3. **Generic greeting** - "Dear Customer" instead of your name
4. **Suspicious links** - Hover, don't click. Check URL
5. **Spelling/grammar errors**
6. **Request for credentials** - Passwords, OTP
7. **Unexpected attachment**
8. **Too good to be true offer**- Winning thousands 
9. **Mismatched URLs**
10. **Request to bypass security**

Rule: STOP - LOOK - THINK before you click.
