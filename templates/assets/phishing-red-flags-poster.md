# 🚨 STOP - PHISHING RED FLAGS POSTER

Print this for your office!

| BEFORE YOU CLICK - CHECK |


1. Who sent it? Is the email correct?
2. Hover over link - does it look real?
3. Urgent? Threat? Too good to be true?
4. Asking for password? -> IT will NEVER ask!
5. Got attachment you didn't expect? Don't open!

IF SUSPICIOUS:
> DON'T CLICK
> REPORT TO IT
> DELETE

Remember: STOP. LOOK. THINK.
